## Executive Summary

This document establishes the hierarchical decision framework for an interactive decision tree that will serve as the central navigational tool for Practice Managers and Practice Owners managing rural generalist training posts. The architecture synthesizes intelligence from the restructured 6-part handbook and embeds comprehensive knowledge of NTCER 2026.1 compliance requirements across four competing priority domains.

**Critical Design Principle:** This decision tree does not gloss over conflicts. It explicitly surfaces tensions between competing priorities and provides frameworks for ethical, strategic decision-making when trade-offs are necessary.

---

## 1. Primary Decision Categories

The decision tree is organized around the **Training Post Lifecycle Model** with four distinct phases, each containing multiple decision pathways:

### 1.1 Pre-Placement Phase
**Primary User Question:** *"How do I prepare my practice to become/remain an accredited training post?"*

**Tier-1 Decision Branches:**
- **A. Practice Accreditation Readiness** → Are we ready to apply for/renew accreditation?
- **B. Supervisor Capability Assessment** → Do we have appropriately accredited supervisors?
- **C. Cultural Safety & Specialist Context Preparation** → Are we equipped for Aboriginal Medical Service, Cultural Mentors, or AST/Hospital Post requirements?
- **D. Business Sustainability Planning** → Can we financially and operationally sustain a training post?

### 1.2 Onboarding Phase (First 8 Weeks)
**Primary User Question:** *"How do I successfully onboard a new registrar?"*

**Tier-1 Decision Branches:**
- **E. Registrar Placement Workflow** → How do we navigate the open-market system and TPA documentation?
- **F. Mutual Fit & Interview Process** → What screening and rostering arrangements are required?
- **G. Onboarding Action Plan & Checklist** → What administrative tasks must be completed in the first 8 weeks?
- **H. Week 5 Check-In & Early Support** → How do we ensure early semester integration and teaching schedule verification?

### 1.3 During Term Phase (Ongoing Supervision)
**Primary User Question:** *"How do I manage ongoing training delivery and compliance?"*

**Tier-1 Decision Branches:**
- **I. Employment & Financial Compliance** → Are we meeting NTCER 2026.1 employment requirements?
- **J. Supervision Model Implementation** → Are we providing appropriate supervision (Principal vs Additional, on-site vs off-site)?
- **K. Teaching Requirements & Scheduling** → Are we protecting teaching time and meeting educational obligations?
- **L. Educational Leave Management** → How do we coordinate mandatory vs study leave?
- **M. ACRRM Fellowship Education Programme** → Are registrars accessing required AFEP courses (including REST)?

### 1.4 End of Term Phase (Monitoring & Retention)
**Primary User Question:** *"How do I ensure quality outcomes and registrar retention?"*

**Tier-1 Decision Branches:**
- **N. Administrative Timeline & Reporting** → Are we meeting mid-semester (Week 13) and end-of-semester (Week 26) reporting requirements?
- **O. Supporting Assessment** → How do we coordinate Supervisor Reports and Workplace-Based Assessments (MiniCEX, Case-Based Discussion)?
- **P. Respectful Workplaces & Wellbeing** → Are we maintaining policies for bullying prevention, cultural safety, and registrar wellbeing?
- **Q. Troubleshooting & Escalation** → When do we engage TPA/Medical Educator support or formal complaints procedures?
- **R. Retention Strategies** → How do we support post-Fellowship pathways and long-term rural retention?

## 2. Logical Decision Sequences

Each tier-1 branch follows a standardized logical sequence to ensure Practice Managers can navigate efficiently:

### Standard Decision Node Sequence

```
ENTRY POINT
    ↓
CONTEXT ASSESSMENT
    ↓
COMPLIANCE CHECK (NTCER 2026.1 clauses)
    ↓
PRIORITY CONFLICT DETECTION
    ↓
DECISION OPTIONS (with consequence frameworks)
    ↓
STAKEHOLDER ENGAGEMENT TRIGGERS
    ↓
ACTION PATHWAY
    ↓
DOCUMENTATION & FOLLOW-UP
```

### Example: Decision Branch E - Registrar Placement Workflow

**Entry Point:** Practice Manager receives notification of registrar interest

**Context Assessment:**
- Is this AGPT or RGTS 2026+ pathway?
- What is our current capacity/ratio?
- Do we have an accredited supervisor available?

**Compliance Check:**
- NTCER 2026.1 Clause: Open-market system requirements
- TPA documentation obligations
- Capacity/ratio considerations per accreditation standards

**Priority Conflict Detection:**
- **Compliance vs Business Sustainability:** Taking a registrar may be financially challenging but required for accreditation maintenance
- **Training Quality vs Operational Efficiency:** Onboarding disrupts practice workflows but is essential for registrar experience
- **Registrar Experience vs Practice Capacity:** Accepting a registrar when supervisor time is limited may compromise both training quality and registrar satisfaction

**Decision Options:**
1. Accept placement immediately (if all conditions met)
2. Negotiate delayed start date (if supervisor availability is pending)
3. Decline placement with TPA notification (if capacity/ratio exceeded or supervisor unavailable)
4. Accept conditionally pending business sustainability assessment

**Stakeholder Engagement Triggers:**
- Consult TPA before declining placement
- Engage Practice Owner/Board for financial sustainability assessment
- Coordinate with existing supervisor(s) regarding capacity

**Action Pathway:**
- Complete TPA documentation
- Initiate Mutual Fit & Interview Process (Branch F)
- Prepare Onboarding Action Plan (Branch G)

**Documentation & Follow-Up:**
- Record decision in practice management system
- Set calendar reminders for Week 5 Check-In (Branch H)
- Update accreditation records

## 3. Decision Point Hierarchy

### Tier-1 Questions (Major Pathway Routing)
These are the primary questions that route users into major branches (A-R listed above). Tier-1 questions correspond to the lifecycle phase and major administrative category.

**Example Tier-1 Questions:**
- "We're considering becoming a training post. Where do we start?" → Branch A
- "A registrar has just started. What do we need to do in the first 8 weeks?" → Branch G
- "How do we handle a registrar's request for study leave?" → Branch L
- "We're having issues with a registrar. What are our options?" → Branch Q

### Tier-2 Questions (Sub-Category Navigation)
Within each tier-1 branch, tier-2 questions guide users to specific compliance requirements, processes, or decision points.

**Example Tier-2 Questions (within Branch I - Employment & Financial Compliance):**
- "What employment template must we use?" → NTCER 2026.1 Mandatory Use of GPSA/GPRA Templates
- "How do we calculate working hours and administration time?" → Key NTCER Provisions for Practice Managers
- "What are the superannuation requirements?" → Remuneration & Business Sustainability
- "How do we access funding?" → Funding Access (AGPT & RGTS 2026+)

### Tier-3 Questions (Specific Compliance/Process Details)
These are granular questions that provide specific guidance, templates, or step-by-step instructions.

**Example Tier-3 Questions (within Branch I → NTCER 2026.1 Mandatory Templates):**
- "Where do I find the GPSA/GPRA template?" → Link to resource
- "What clauses must be included for AGPT/RGTS 2026+ registrars?" → Checklist of mandatory clauses
- "What happens if we use a non-compliant contract?" → Consequence framework and remediation pathway

## 4. Natural Workflow Integration

The decision tree follows the natural administrative workflows that Practice Managers experience:

### Workflow 1: New Training Post Establishment
**Lifecycle Phases:** Pre-Placement → Onboarding  
**Decision Sequence:** A → B → C (if applicable) → D → E → F → G → H

### Workflow 2: Registrar Placement (Existing Training Post)
**Lifecycle Phases:** Pre-Placement → Onboarding  
**Decision Sequence:** E → F → G → H

### Workflow 3: Ongoing Term Management
**Lifecycle Phases:** During Term  
**Decision Sequence:** I → J → K → L → M (cyclical, as issues arise)

### Workflow 4: Mid-Term & End-of-Term Reporting
**Lifecycle Phases:** During Term → End of Term  
**Decision Sequence:** N → O → P (ongoing) → Q (if needed)

### Workflow 5: Retention & Post-Fellowship Planning
**Lifecycle Phases:** End of Term  
**Decision Sequence:** R

### Workflow 6: Problem Resolution
**Lifecycle Phases:** Any phase  
**Decision Sequence:** P → Q (can be triggered from any other branch)

## 6. Cross-Referencing Architecture

The decision tree employs bidirectional cross-referencing to ensure users can navigate between related decision points:

### Forward References
When a decision in one branch triggers actions in another branch, the tree provides explicit forward references.

**Example:**
- Branch E (Registrar Placement Workflow) → Forward reference to Branch F (Mutual Fit & Interview Process)
- Branch G (Onboarding Action Plan) → Forward reference to Branch I (Employment & Financial Compliance)

### Backward References
When a decision requires prerequisite information from an earlier branch, the tree provides backward references.

**Example:**
- Branch L (Educational Leave Management) → Backward reference to Branch I (Employment & Financial Compliance) for NTCER provisions on leave accrual
- Branch Q (Troubleshooting & Escalation) → Backward reference to Branch P (Respectful Workplaces) for policy context

### Lateral References
When competing priorities create tension, the tree provides lateral references to related decision points.

**Example:**
- Branch I (Employment & Financial Compliance) ↔ Branch D (Business Sustainability Planning)
- Branch K (Teaching Requirements) ↔ Branch J (Supervision Model Implementation)

## 8. Scalability and Future Expansion

The hierarchical framework is designed to accommodate future expansion:

### Expansion Points
- **New NTCER Clauses:** Can be integrated into existing branches as tier-3 questions
- **New Funding Models:** Can be added to Branch I (Employment & Financial Compliance) and Branch D (Business Sustainability Planning)
- **New Stakeholder Requirements:** Can be integrated as new tier-2 questions within relevant branches
- **New Training Pathways:** Can be accommodated by adding pathway-specific tier-2 questions

### Modular Design
Each decision branch is self-contained, allowing for independent updates without disrupting the overall architecture.

## 9. User Entry Points

Practice Managers can enter the decision tree through multiple pathways:

### Entry by Lifecycle Phase
- "I'm in the pre-placement phase" → Branches A-D
- "I'm onboarding a registrar" → Branches E-H
- "I'm managing an ongoing placement" → Branches I-M
- "I'm preparing for reporting/end of term" → Branches N-R

### Entry by Topic
- "I need help with compliance" → Branch I
- "I need help with supervision" → Branch J
- "I need help with accreditation" → Branch A
- "I have a problem/conflict" → Branch Q

### Entry by Urgency
- "I have a registrar starting next week" → Branch G
- "I have a reporting deadline" → Branch N
- "I have a registrar complaint" → Branch Q
- "I need to decide on a placement offer" → Branch E

### Entry by Handbook Section
Users can also enter via the handbook structure (Parts 1-6), with the decision tree providing the interactive navigation layer.
