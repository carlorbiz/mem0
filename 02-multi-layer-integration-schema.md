## Executive Summary

This document defines how **four competing priority layers** are integrated into each decision node of the ACRRM Practice Manager Decision Tree. The schema ensures that NTCER 2026.1 compliance requirements, training quality considerations, business sustainability factors, and registrar experience/retention data are embedded at relevant decision points, creating a comprehensive, multi-dimensional decision support tool.

**Critical Design Principle:** The integration schema makes competing priorities visible and explicit, rather than hiding them. Practice Managers must see where priorities align and where they conflict.

---

## 1. The Four Priority Layers

### Layer 1: NTCER Compliance Requirements
**Nature:** Mandatory regulatory obligations  
**Authority:** National Terms and Conditions for Employment of Registrars (NTCER 2026.1)  
**Consequence of Non-Compliance:** Risk to accreditation, funding ineligibility, legal liability  
**Color Code (for visual representation):** Red

**Key NTCER 2026.1 Clauses Integrated:**
- Mandatory use of GPSA/GPRA employment templates (AGPT/RGTS 2026+)
- Working hours and administration time provisions
- Leave accrual and notice period requirements
- International medical graduate (IMG) registrar considerations
- Superannuation requirements (12%)
- Payroll tax obligations

### Layer 2: Training Quality Delivery
**Nature:** Educational outcomes and registrar development  
**Authority:** ACRRM curriculum requirements, AFEP programme, supervision standards  
**Consequence of Non-Compliance:** Poor registrar outcomes, reduced retention, reputational damage  
**Color Code (for visual representation):** Blue

**Key Training Quality Factors Integrated:**
- Formal and informal teaching time requirements
- Quarantining teaching time and patient load limits
- Supervision models (Principal vs Additional, on-site vs off-site)
- Educational leave coordination (ACRRM Education Release Table)
- AFEP course access (including REST - Rural Emergency Skills Training)
- Workplace-Based Assessment support (MiniCEX, Case-Based Discussion)
- Supervisor Reports and mid-term check-ins

### Layer 3: Business Sustainability
**Nature:** Operational efficiency and financial viability  
**Authority:** Practice business model, funding structures, workforce planning  
**Consequence of Non-Compliance:** Financial loss, practice closure, inability to sustain training post  
**Color Code (for visual representation):** Green

**Key Business Sustainability Factors Integrated:**
- Base rates and top-up payments (billings/receipts model)
- Funding access (PRODA/HPOS setup, GP Training Incentive Payments, AMS Salary Support, Flexible Funds)
- Capacity/ratio considerations for practice workload
- Rostering and operational efficiency
- Cost-benefit analysis of training post participation
- Long-term workforce planning and succession

### Layer 4: Registrar Experience and Retention
**Nature:** Workforce development and rural retention outcomes  
**Authority:** ACRRM strategic objectives, rural workforce sustainability goals  
**Consequence of Non-Compliance:** Registrar attrition, failure to achieve rural retention outcomes  
**Color Code (for visual representation):** Purple

**Key Registrar Experience/Retention Factors Integrated:**
- Respectful workplace policies (bullying prevention, cultural safety)
- Registrar wellbeing initiatives
- Community integration support
- Post-Fellowship pathway planning
- Business of general practice concepts for long-term rural retention
- Positive onboarding experiences (Week 5 check-in, early semester support)
- Mutual fit and interview process quality

## 3. Priority Alignment Analysis

At each decision node, the integration schema includes an explicit analysis of where the four priorities align and where they conflict.

### Example: Decision Node I.1 - Selecting Employment Contract Template

**CONTEXT:**  
Practice Manager must select an employment contract template for a new AGPT/RGTS 2026+ registrar.

**LAYER 1: NTCER COMPLIANCE**  
- **Relevant NTCER 2026.1 Clauses:** Mandatory use of GPSA/GPRA templates for AGPT/RGTS 2026+ registrars
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  - ✓ Use GPSA template (for GPSA-employed registrars)
  - ✓ Use GPRA template (for practice-employed registrars)
  - ✓ Include all mandatory clauses (working hours, admin time, leave, superannuation)
- **Compliance Risk if Not Met:** Practice ineligible for funding, registrar placement may be withdrawn, accreditation at risk

**LAYER 2: TRAINING QUALITY**  
- **Educational Objectives:** Contract should protect teaching time and ensure registrar has adequate support
- **Supervisor Responsibilities:** Contract should clarify supervisor availability and teaching commitments
- **Quality Risk if Not Met:** Registrar may not receive adequate teaching time, impacting educational outcomes

**LAYER 3: BUSINESS SUSTAINABILITY**  
- **Financial Considerations:** GPSA template = GPSA pays registrar, practice receives top-up; GPRA template = practice pays registrar, receives funding
- **Operational Efficiency:** GPSA template reduces practice administrative burden (payroll managed by GPSA)
- **Sustainability Risk if Not Met:** Incorrect template choice may create unexpected financial obligations or administrative complexity

**LAYER 4: REGISTRAR EXPERIENCE & RETENTION**  
- **Registrar Wellbeing Factors:** Clear contract terms reduce uncertainty and support positive experience
- **Retention Indicators:** Fair employment terms and protected teaching time support long-term rural retention
- **Retention Risk if Not Met:** Poor contract terms may lead to registrar dissatisfaction and early departure

**PRIORITY ALIGNMENT ANALYSIS:**

✅ **Where Priorities Align:**
- Using the correct NTCER-compliant template satisfies compliance (Layer 1), protects teaching time (Layer 2), clarifies financial arrangements (Layer 3), and supports registrar confidence (Layer 4)
- All four priorities converge on the importance of using the correct template

⚠️ **Where Priorities Conflict:**
- **GPSA vs GPRA Choice:** 
  - Layer 1 (Compliance): Either template is compliant if used correctly
  - Layer 3 (Business Sustainability): GPSA template reduces administrative burden but may limit practice control over employment terms; GPRA template gives practice more control but increases payroll complexity
  - Layer 4 (Registrar Experience): GPSA employment may provide more stability (national employer); GPRA employment may provide more flexibility (practice-specific arrangements)
- **Resolution Framework:** Practice Manager should assess administrative capacity and preference for employment control, then select template accordingly. Both are compliant; choice depends on business model preference.

**DECISION OPTIONS:**
1. **Use GPSA Template** → Compliance ✓, Quality ✓, Sustainability ✓ (lower admin burden), Retention ✓
2. **Use GPRA Template** → Compliance ✓, Quality ✓, Sustainability ✓ (more control), Retention ✓
3. **Use Non-Compliant Template** → Compliance ✗, Quality ?, Sustainability ✗, Retention ✗ (DO NOT CHOOSE)

## 8. Conflict Recognition Triggers

The integration schema includes automated conflict recognition triggers that alert Practice Managers when competing priorities create tension:

### Trigger 1: Compliance vs Business Sustainability
**Activated When:** NTCER compliance requirement creates financial burden or operational complexity

**Example Decision Nodes:**
- I.1 (Employment Contract Selection) - GPSA vs GPRA choice
- I.4 (Payroll and Superannuation Setup) - 12% superannuation requirement
- L.1 (Educational Leave Requests) - Mandatory leave impacts rostering

**Conflict Disclosure Message:**  
⚠️ "This decision involves a trade-off between regulatory compliance and business sustainability. While compliance is mandatory, you may need to adjust your business model or seek additional funding to maintain financial viability."

### Trigger 2: Training Quality vs Operational Efficiency
**Activated When:** Educational requirements reduce practice productivity or revenue

**Example Decision Nodes:**
- K.1 (Teaching Time Protection) - Quarantining teaching time reduces billable hours
- K.2 (Patient Load Management) - Limiting patient load impacts revenue
- O.2 (WBA Coordination) - Assessment activities require supervisor time

**Conflict Disclosure Message:**  
⚠️ "This decision involves a trade-off between training quality and operational efficiency. Prioritizing educational outcomes may temporarily reduce practice productivity, but supports long-term registrar development and retention."

### Trigger 3: Registrar Experience vs Practice Capacity
**Activated When:** Registrar support needs exceed practice resources

**Example Decision Nodes:**
- E.1 (Placement Capacity Assessment) - Accepting registrar when supervisor time is limited
- J.3 (Off-Site Supervision Arrangements) - Registrar prefers on-site supervision but practice cannot provide
- M.1 (AFEP Course Access) - Registrar course attendance requires practice coverage

**Conflict Disclosure Message:**  
⚠️ "This decision involves a trade-off between registrar experience and practice capacity. If your practice cannot adequately support the registrar's needs, consider declining the placement or negotiating alternative arrangements to avoid compromising both training quality and registrar satisfaction."

### Trigger 4: Compliance vs Training Quality
**Activated When:** Minimum compliance standards may not achieve optimal educational outcomes

**Example Decision Nodes:**
- J.1 (Supervision Model Selection) - Additional Supervisor model meets compliance but may reduce teaching continuity
- L.2 (Study Leave Coordination) - Minimum leave entitlement may not support registrar's educational goals

**Conflict Disclosure Message:**  
⚠️ "This decision involves a trade-off between minimum compliance standards and optimal training quality. While the minimum standard is acceptable, exceeding it may improve registrar outcomes and retention."

## 9. Multi-Layer Decision Consequence Framework

Each decision option in the tree includes a multi-layer consequence analysis:

### Consequence Framework Template

```
DECISION OPTION: [Description]
├── LAYER 1 CONSEQUENCES (Compliance)
│   ├── Immediate: [Short-term compliance impact]
│   └── Long-term: [Ongoing compliance obligations or risks]
├── LAYER 2 CONSEQUENCES (Training Quality)
│   ├── Immediate: [Short-term educational impact]
│   └── Long-term: [Impact on registrar development and outcomes]
├── LAYER 3 CONSEQUENCES (Business Sustainability)
│   ├── Immediate: [Short-term financial or operational impact]
│   └── Long-term: [Impact on practice viability and sustainability]
├── LAYER 4 CONSEQUENCES (Registrar Experience & Retention)
│   ├── Immediate: [Short-term impact on registrar satisfaction]
│   └── Long-term: [Impact on rural retention and workforce outcomes]
└── OVERALL RISK RATING: [Low / Medium / High]
```

### Example: Decision Node E.1 - Placement Capacity Assessment

**DECISION OPTION 1: Accept Registrar Placement (when at capacity limit)**

**LAYER 1 CONSEQUENCES (Compliance):**
- Immediate: ✓ Maintains accreditation status, meets TPA expectations
- Long-term: ⚠️ Risk of exceeding capacity/ratio if another placement is offered

**LAYER 2 CONSEQUENCES (Training Quality):**
- Immediate: ⚠️ Supervisor time may be stretched thin
- Long-term: ⚠️ Risk of reduced teaching quality if supervisor is over-committed

**LAYER 3 CONSEQUENCES (Business Sustainability):**
- Immediate: ✓ Secures funding and top-up payments
- Long-term: ⚠️ May strain practice resources if workload increases

**LAYER 4 CONSEQUENCES (Registrar Experience & Retention):**
- Immediate: ✓ Registrar has placement opportunity
- Long-term: ⚠️ Risk of poor registrar experience if support is inadequate

**OVERALL RISK RATING:** Medium  
**RECOMMENDATION:** Accept placement only if supervisor confirms capacity and practice commits to protecting teaching time.

---

**DECISION OPTION 2: Decline Registrar Placement (when at capacity limit)**

**LAYER 1 CONSEQUENCES (Compliance):**
- Immediate: ⚠️ Must notify TPA with justification
- Long-term: ⚠️ Risk to accreditation if pattern of declining placements emerges

**LAYER 2 CONSEQUENCES (Training Quality):**
- Immediate: ✓ Protects quality of training for existing registrars
- Long-term: ✓ Maintains supervisor capacity for high-quality teaching

**LAYER 3 CONSEQUENCES (Business Sustainability):**
- Immediate: ✗ Foregoes funding and top-up payments
- Long-term: ✓ Protects practice from over-commitment and burnout

**LAYER 4 CONSEQUENCES (Registrar Experience & Retention):**
- Immediate: ✗ Registrar must seek alternative placement
- Long-term: ✓ Ensures existing registrars receive adequate support

**OVERALL RISK RATING:** Medium  
**RECOMMENDATION:** Decline placement if supervisor capacity is genuinely exceeded, but engage TPA to discuss future capacity planning.

## 10. Visual Integration Schema

The decision tree will use visual cues to indicate which priority layers are active at each decision node:

### Color-Coding System
- **Red Border:** NTCER Compliance requirements present
- **Blue Border:** Training Quality considerations present
- **Green Border:** Business Sustainability factors present
- **Purple Border:** Registrar Experience/Retention factors present
- **Multi-Color Border:** Multiple priority layers intersect (conflict potential)

### Icon System
- 🔴 **Compliance Icon:** Indicates mandatory NTCER requirement
- 🔵 **Quality Icon:** Indicates educational outcome consideration
- 🟢 **Sustainability Icon:** Indicates financial or operational factor
- 🟣 **Retention Icon:** Indicates registrar experience or retention factor
- ⚠️ **Conflict Icon:** Indicates competing priorities require trade-off decision

### Priority Alignment Indicator
- ✅ **Aligned:** All four priorities support the same decision
- ⚖️ **Balanced:** Priorities require careful balancing but can be reconciled
- ⚠️ **Conflict:** Priorities are in tension; trade-offs necessary

## 11. Implementation Notes

### Data Sources for Layer Integration
- **Layer 1 (Compliance):** NTCER 2026.1 document, GPSA/GPRA templates, accreditation standards
- **Layer 2 (Training Quality):** ACRRM curriculum, AFEP programme requirements, supervision standards
- **Layer 3 (Business Sustainability):** Funding guidelines (PRODA/HPOS), practice financial models, capacity planning tools
- **Layer 4 (Registrar Experience):** Retention research, registrar feedback data, wellbeing frameworks

### Update and Maintenance
- NTCER clauses: Review annually or when NTCER is updated
- Training quality factors: Review when ACRRM curriculum changes
- Business sustainability factors: Review when funding models change
- Registrar experience factors: Review based on registrar feedback and retention data
